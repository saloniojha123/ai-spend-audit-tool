# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy: AI Spend Audit Tool

## Phase 1: The First 100 Users (Organic Growth)
To acquire our first 100 users for $0, we will focus on high-intent communities where AI spend is a common "pain point."

* **LinkedIn "Audit" Campaign**: Post a daily "AI Savings Tip" and offer free manual audits using the tool to anyone who comments.
* **Product Hunt Launch**: Target the "Developer Tools" category to reach early adopters and SaaS founders.
* **Niche Communities**: Engage in Reddit (r/SaaS, r/OpenAI) and IndieHackers by sharing "Cost Benchmark" data found during our research.

## Phase 2: Conversion Hook
The "Claim Savings via Credex" button acts as our primary lead magnet. By providing a personalized AI summary first, we build trust before asking for the user's email.

## Success Metric
* **North Star**: Number of completed audits that result in a "Lead Submission" (Email capture).
* **Goal**: 15% conversion rate from "Audit View" to "Lead Captured."
