# Landing Page Copy Strategy

## Value Proposition
The copy focuses on "Loss Aversion"—pointing out that the user is currently "burning cash."

## Target Audience
- **CTOs/Founders**: Focused on the bottom line and efficiency.
- **Operations Managers**: Focused on consolidating messy billing.

## Tone of Voice
- **Authoritative**: "Audit Engine," "Benchmarks," "Analysis."
- **Urgent**: "Stop Burning Cash," "Claim Immediately."
- **Professional**: High-contrast, clean language, and clear CTAs.
