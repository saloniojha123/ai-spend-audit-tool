# Unit Economics: Credex Arbitrage Model

## The Revenue Model
Credex operates on a "Credit Arbitrage" model. We source unused AI credits from enterprises at a discount and provide them to high-growth startups.

* **User Pain**: The user is currently paying "Retail" prices (e.g., $20/mo for ChatGPT Plus).
* **The Solution**: Credex provides access to the same API capabilities or seat licenses at a 20-30% discount.
* **Credex Margin**: If Credex acquires credits at a 50% discount and sells them at a 30% discount, we maintain a **20% gross margin** on every dollar of AI spend managed through our platform.

## Lead Valuation
Every "Lead" captured by this tool (Email + Company Name + Audit Spend) is valued based on their **Potential Annual Spend**. 
* **High-Value Lead**: Any user spending >$500/mo on AI tools ($6,000/year).
* **Total Addressable Savings**: Based on our Audit Engine, we estimate a 30% reduction in waste for the average mid-sized team.
